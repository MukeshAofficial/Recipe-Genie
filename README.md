# RecipeGenie

RecipeGenie is a responsive web application designed to help users discover recipes, plan meals, and enhance their cooking experience with a variety of innovative features.

---

## Features

### 
- **Meals**: Browse a variety of recipes.
- **Meal Planner**: Plan your meals for the week with ease.
- **Cooking Assistant**: Access an AI-powered chef for cooking tips.
- **Preferences**: Customize your dietary preferences for tailored results.
- **Bookmarks**: Save your favorite recipes for quick access.
- **Generate Recipes**: Create customized recipes using selected ingredients and preferences.
- **Meal Planning**: Craft personalized 7-day meal plans.
- **Cooking Assistant**: Real-time AI chef to answer questions and provide tips.
- **Upload Ingredients**: Use images of ingredients to get recipe suggestions.
- **Food Image Recognition**: Identify dishes from images and find related recipes.
- **Set Preferences**: Adjust settings for dietary restrictions or cuisine preferences.
- **Voice Assistance**: Hands-free recipe guidance via voice instructions.
- **Price Comparison**: Compare ingredient prices across sources.
